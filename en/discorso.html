A typescript that Natale Capellaro presumably wrote in preparation for a speech to celebrate Teresio’s promotion to Central Director, effective on January 1, 1960. Capellaro had held the position of Central Director in 1952. Source: personal archive.

The words of Elserino Piol, manager at Olivetti, from his book Il sogno di un’impresa. Dall’Olivetti al venture capital: una vita nell’information technology (“The dream of an enterprise. From Olivetti to venture capital, a life in information technology”) (Il Sole 24 Ore, 2004), chapters Olivetti nel 1960 (“Olivetti in 1960”), pp. 19–25, and Strategia prodotti nella “nuova” Olivetti (“Product Strategy in the ‘new’ Olivetti”), pp. 75–79.

The words of Sandro Sartor, human resources manager at Olivetti, from his book Via Jervis, n. 11. Alla ricerca dell’Olivetti perduta (“11 Jervis Street. Looking for the lost Olivetti”) (Manni Editori, 2005), chapter Il regno di Capellaro (“Capellaro’s kingdom”), pp. 45–53.

The memories of Luciano Banchelli from his social media page (links 1 and 2). Hired in 1936 through the Mechanical School and employed at Olivetti for forty years, he is the author of several memoirs in which he also recalls his years at the company.
